Preferences

Language / 界面语言

Switch the interface between English and Simplified Chinese. The language pack is stored separately in your library folder.

Library

The folder that holds every one of your projects. This folder is the part that travels: put it in iCloud Drive and both your Macs open the same projects — just open a project on one Mac at a time, because two at once makes iCloud pick a winner. Everything else on this page is remembered on this Mac only.

Your name on exports

Shared across all your projects. Your name appears as an author credit on export covers (Word, PDF overview, For AI chat). Leave blank to show no credit. Each project can also turn the credit off on its own, in Project info.

Canvas
